# Code of Conduct

LoopForge is committed to a welcoming, harassment-free community for everyone,
regardless of background, identity, experience level, or point of view.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Assume good intent while accepting responsibility for impact.
- Respect privacy, security boundaries, and responsible-disclosure requests.
- Make room for people with different levels of expertise.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, deliberate
intimidation, doxxing, and sustained disruption are not acceptable in project
spaces or when representing the project publicly.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the maintainer through the contact options
on [Ruben Sanchez's GitHub profile](https://github.com/Rsan0948). For security
vulnerabilities, use the separate process in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions, comments, or other participation
that violates this policy, and may temporarily or permanently restrict
participation. Reports will be handled as confidentially as practical. Anyone
reviewing a report in which they have a conflict of interest must recuse
themselves.

This policy applies in all project spaces and in public spaces when someone is
acting as a representative of LoopForge.
